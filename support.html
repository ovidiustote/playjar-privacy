If you need help with Play Jar, have a question, or want to report a problem, you can contact us by email.

What to include

To help us understand your issue, please include:

  • your device model
  • your iOS version
  • the Play Jar app version
  • a short description of the problem
  • screenshots if helpful

Purchases

Play Jar’s Lifetime Unlock is handled securely by Apple through StoreKit. If you have already purchased Lifetime Unlock, you can use Restore Purchases inside the app.
